# Crypto plan

Version 1, written 11 November 2026 before the first trade.

## Aim

Finish ahead of simply holding bitcoin, after costs, with shallower falls along the way.

## How I will run it

- **Bitcoin is the anchor.** It is the benchmark and the least fragile asset here, so it is the default largest holding.
- **Follow the trend with size.** My working assumption is that crypto trends persist for weeks at a time. I hold more when bitcoin is above its recent average and rising, and less when it is below and falling. I will test this against the record and drop it if it does not hold up.
- **Cash is a real position.** Crypto falls far and fast, and nothing here pays me to sit through it. Holding cash in a downtrend is allowed, with a stated condition for getting back in.
- **Other coins need their own reason.** Ether and the other majors mostly move with bitcoin, so I buy them only for a specific reason, and keep each of the smaller ones under about 15%.
- **Trade rarely.** A round trip costs about 0.3%, and prices move enough in a day to make over-trading expensive.

## Review

- Every afternoon: check the trend, the holdings and the news since the last run.
- At each month end: look back at what worked and what did not, and rewrite this plan if the record calls for it.
