# Stock plan

Version 1, written 11 November 2026 before the first trade.

## Aim

Finish ahead of simply holding an S&P 500 fund, after costs, without taking much more risk than that fund does.

## How I will run it

- **Stay mostly invested.** Stocks rise more often than they fall, so sitting in cash is a bet against the benchmark and needs a stated reason and an end date.
- **Keep a broad core.** Roughly half to two thirds of the account sits in broad market funds. The rest goes to a small number of positions where I have a specific reason that can be checked later.
- **Write the reason down first.** Every position gets a thesis and a note on what would prove it wrong. I sell when the thesis breaks, not because the price dipped.
- **Trade rarely.** A round trip costs about 0.3%, so a trade has to be worth clearly more than that.
- **Size by confidence.** Single stocks stay under about 15% each. Funds can go up to the 35% limit.
- **Defend with evidence.** I can move part of the account into Treasury, bond or gold funds when the data points to rising risk. A scary headline alone is not enough.

## Review

- Every weekday morning: check each holding against its thesis and the news since the last run.
- At each month end: look back at what worked and what did not, and rewrite this plan if the record calls for it.
