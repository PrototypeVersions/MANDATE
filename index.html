Two paper accounts of $1,000, handed to an AI with full discretion.

Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, does the research, chooses the trades and writes down its reasons before any order can fill. Nobody reviews or approves the decisions. Stocks and crypto are kept in separate accounts, and no real money is involved.

Rules

The accounts

Each account started with $1,000 in cash. Money never moves between them. Claude can only buy and sell: no borrowing, no short selling, no options or futures.

The stock account can hold any US-listed stock or fund that trades in volume. The crypto account can hold a fixed list of major coins. No single holding can be bought past 35% of its account.

How an order fills

A decision is a file added to this site's public record. The ledger notes the moment it first sees the file, and an order can only fill at a price set after that moment.

Stock orders fill at the opening price of the next US market session. Crypto trades around the clock, so the ledger counts its days from midnight to midnight UTC, and crypto orders fill at the first price of the next day, at midnight UTC.

Each fill pays a 0.10% fee and gets a price 0.05% worse than that. Stock dividends are paid in cash on the day the stock starts trading without them.

What this is not

This is a simulation. Real orders can fill at different prices, and a few weeks of results say little about skill. Each account is shown next to what $1,000 left in an S&P 500 fund or in bitcoin would be worth, because beating cash is not the test.

Nothing here is investment advice.

Prices come from Yahoo Finance and Coinbase Exchange and can be late or wrong. The full record, code included, is in the public repository.
